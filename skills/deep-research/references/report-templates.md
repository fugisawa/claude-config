# Research Report Templates

## Standard Research Report

```markdown
# [Research Topic]

## Executive Summary

[2-3 paragraphs capturing:
- The core question addressed
- Key findings (the most important 3-5 points)
- Main conclusions
- Critical caveats or limitations]

## Research Questions

This research sought to answer:
1. [Primary question]
2. [Secondary question]
3. [Additional questions as relevant]

## Methodology

**Research approach:** [Describe the systematic approach taken]

**Source types consulted:**
- [List source categories with counts, e.g., "12 academic papers"]
- [Government/official sources]
- [News sources]
- [Expert analyses]
- [Primary documents]

**Time frame:** [When research was conducted, date range of sources]

**Limitations:** [Brief note on access limitations, time constraints, etc.]

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: [Title]

[Detailed explanation with source attribution]

**Confidence level:** [High/Medium/Low]
**Sources:** [List key sources]

### Finding 2: [Title]

[Continue pattern...]

## Analysis

### [Theme/Dimension 1]

[Synthesized analysis connecting multiple findings]

### [Theme/Dimension 2]

[Continue as needed...]

## Areas of Uncertainty

### Unresolved Questions
- [Question that remains unanswered and why]
- [Areas where sources conflict without resolution]

### Information Gaps
- [What data/sources were not available]
- [What would strengthen conclusions]

## Conclusions

### Primary Conclusions
[Direct answers to research questions with confidence levels]

### Implications
[What these findings mean for the user's needs]

### Recommendations for Further Research
[If applicable, what additional investigation would help]

## Sources

### Primary Sources
- [Source with access date]

### Academic/Research
- [Source with access date]

### News/Journalism
- [Source with access date]

### Expert Analysis
- [Source with access date]

### Official/Government
- [Source with access date]
```

## Quick Research Brief

For shorter research needs:

```markdown
# Research Brief: [Topic]

**Question:** [What was asked]

**Key Findings:**
1. [Most important finding] (Confidence: High/Medium/Low)
2. [Second finding] (Confidence: High/Medium/Low)
3. [Third finding] (Confidence: High/Medium/Low)

**Bottom Line:** [1-2 sentence answer to the question]

**Caveats:** [Most important limitations]

**Key Sources:** [3-5 most important sources]
```

## Comparative Analysis Report

```markdown
# Comparative Analysis: [Subject A] vs [Subject B]

## Overview

[Brief introduction to what's being compared and why]

## Comparison Summary

| Dimension | [Subject A] | [Subject B] | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| [Dim 1]   | [Value]     | [Value]     | [Context] |
| [Dim 2]   | [Value]     | [Value]     | [Context] |

## Detailed Analysis

### [Dimension 1]

**[Subject A]:** [Analysis with sources]

**[Subject B]:** [Analysis with sources]

**Comparison:** [How they differ and why it matters]

### [Continue for each dimension...]

## Conclusions

[Which is "better" depends on criteria - explain tradeoffs]

## Sources

[Organized by subject being compared]
```

```markdown
- Q2: [Question]

| Time | Query | Results | Key Findings |
|------|-------|---------|--------------|
| [Time] | [Search query] | [# results, top sources] | [What was learned] |

## Source Inventory

| Source | Type | Credibility | Key Claims | Notes |
|--------|------|-------------|------------|-------|

```

## Citation Formatting

**Academic/Journal:**
Author(s). "Title." Journal Name, vol. X, no. Y, Year, pp. XX-XX.

**News Article:**
Author. "Title." Publication, Date. URL

**Government/Official:**
Agency. "Document Title." Date. URL

**Website:**
Organization/Author. "Page Title." Site Name, Date if available. URL. Accessed [Date].

**Report:**
Organization. "Report Title." Date. URL
